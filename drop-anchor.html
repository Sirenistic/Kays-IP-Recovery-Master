DROP ANCHOR

A simple grounding skill for handling difficult thoughts, feelings, urges and sensations.

WHAT IS DROP ANCHOR?

Dropping Anchor helps you step out of autopilot and reconnect with the present moment when difficult thoughts, feelings, memories, sensations or urges show up.

It uses three simple steps:

A ACKNOWLEDGE
C CONNECT
E ENGAGE
A

ACKNOWLEDGE

Your thoughts and feelings

Notice what is happening inside you without fighting it. Take a curious, non-judgemental approach to what you are experiencing.

Try putting what you notice into simple words beginning with:

"I'm noticing..."
I'm noticing anxiety.
I'm noticing an urge.
I'm noticing my mind racing.
I'm noticing thoughts about pornography.
I'm noticing feelings of shame.
C

CONNECT

With your body

Bring your attention back to your physical body while continuing to acknowledge what is happening inside you.

Slowly push your feet firmly into the floor.
Straighten your back and sit upright.
Slowly press your fingertips together.
Stretch your arms or neck.
Slowly breathe.
Remember

You are not trying to make the thoughts or feelings disappear. Notice them while also becoming aware of your body in the present moment.

E

ENGAGE

With what you're doing

Look around and reconnect with where you are. Bring your attention back to the activity in front of you.

Notice where you are.
Look at the things around you.
Notice what you can hear.
Feel your body as you continue the activity.
Put your attention on the next useful thing you can do.
