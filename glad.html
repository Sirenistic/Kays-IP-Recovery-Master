G.L.A.D.

Four ways of finding joy and balance.

G.L.A.D. works by paying attention to positive aspects of life that are around you all the time, but which frequently go unnoticed.

TAKE 14 MINUTES
14:00
G

Gratitude

One thing you are grateful for today.

Be as specific as possible. Don't just name the thing — think about what it is about that thing that you appreciate.

Gratitude can be something basic, such as having food and water, sunlight, a body that works well enough, or a roof over your head.

It can also be something significant, such as a devoted relationship, meaningful work, a caring community of friends, or robust health.

Try not to repeat the same item twice. It doesn't need to be a big thing — it just needs to be something you genuinely reflect on.

L

Learning

One new thing you learned today.

Stay curious. Look for something new about yourself, another person, or the world around you.

You might have learned something about yourself, such as noticing an insight or piece of wisdom that you possess.

You might discover something new or interesting about another person, even someone you have known for a long time.

It could simply be a new fact or a new perspective on something. Curiosity and learning can themselves be sources of happiness.

A

Accomplishment

One small thing you accomplished or achieved today.

Your accomplishment doesn't have to be big. Notice something you did for yourself or someone else.

Getting enough sleep.

Choosing not to eat extra sugary foods.

Making your bed or getting dressed in the morning.

Doing anything that moves you, even slightly, toward reaching a long-term goal.

D

Delight

One moment of delight that touched you today.

Look for anything that made you laugh, smile, or brought you a small moment of joy.

Hearing a bird chirp, seeing a colourful flower, or noticing the blue of the sky.

Laughing, tasting food, returning a smile, or hugging a friend or family member.

Even something as simple as noticing the pleasant sensation of water on your hands while washing the dishes can count.
